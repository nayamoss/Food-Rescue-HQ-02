Food Rescue HQ

30-second demo script. Say this.

What it does

Say or snap a photo of the food that's left. The app looks at the picture and counts it, like a friend saying "that's about 100 tacos."

You tap one button. The app phones nearby shelters and asks, "Can you take this?" The first one to press 1 gets it.

Then it phones a volunteer: "Please pick this up here and drop it off there." When they deliver it, they tap Delivered.

The whole time you watch it happen on one screen, like a group chat.

How it's built (if they ask)

One small program runs in the background.

  • An AI that can see counts the food in the photo.
  • An AI that can hear lets you just talk to it.
  • Twilio is the robot phone: it dials people and listens for which button they press.
  • Everything is saved in one little notebook file, and the screen updates by itself.
  • If any piece is missing, the app still works: you type the numbers or press the demo buttons.

The live demo

  1. Rescue tab: say which event this is.
  2. Take a photo, or say "we started with 400 tacos, 100 left."
  3. Check the card, tap Yes, alert partners.
  4. Your phone rings as the shelter. Press 1.
  5. It rings again as the volunteer. Press 1.
  6. Open the volunteer link, tap Delivered. Watch the summary.

If a call doesn't come: account menu → Demo tools → Simulate reply.

Close with

"Food that would have been thrown out gets to people who need it, in minutes, with one tap."
